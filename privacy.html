Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Effective August 24, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Afterdue at afterdue.com (and www.afterdue.com). Afterdue is operated by Afterdue / Abhishek Raj. If you need to reach a person, write support@afterdue.com. We do not publish a street address or phone number.

What Afterdue is

A static website. A free invoice generator that runs in your browser. A $19 digital download — the Get Paid Pack. There is no Afterdue account. Afterdue does not process your clients’ payments, send invoices for you, or contact your clients.

What we collect on this site

Almost nothing.

The invoice form — your name, their name, line items, tax, due date, Zelle / ACH / Venmo instructions — stays in the browser. A draft may be saved in your browser’s local storage so a refresh does not wipe it. This static site does not upload those fields to Afterdue. We do not keep a database of invoices. You can clear the draft from the form, or by clearing site data in the browser.

Afterdue does not set cookies of its own. There is no Afterdue analytics pixel, no account cookie, and no cookie banner, because we are not storing a session on our side.

If you email support@afterdue.com, we will have whatever you put in that message, and we will use it to reply.

If you buy the Get Paid Pack

You check out on Polar (polar.sh). Polar is the merchant of record. Polar takes the card, calculates tax, and emails you the pack. Afterdue does not store card numbers and does not see your full payment card data.

Polar will have the email and payment details you give them at checkout. That collection is governed by Polar’s Privacy Policy and Polar’s Buyer Terms, not by this page.

Polar may share your name, email, and the fact that you bought the pack with Afterdue so we can answer support mail about that order. We use that only for delivery problems and questions about the file. We do not start a newsletter from the purchase.

Third parties that can see a request

  • The host that serves afterdue.com will typically log ordinary web requests (IP address, user agent, pages requested). We do not use those logs to build a marketing profile.
  • The site loads type from Google Fonts. Google may see that request.
  • Polar, if you open checkout.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run ads on Afterdue.
  • We do not have Afterdue user accounts.
  • We do not store invoice contents on our servers.

How long we keep things

Invoice form data lasts in your browser until you clear the draft or site data. We do not hold a copy.

Support email lasts as long as the thread is useful, then we delete it in the ordinary course of mail. Purchase records live with Polar.

Your choices

You can use the invoice tool without sending us anything. You can decline to buy. You can email support@afterdue.com to ask what we have from you — usually nothing, or an email you sent.

Children

Afterdue is for working adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
