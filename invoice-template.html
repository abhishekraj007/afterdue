What belongs on the page

A freelance invoice is a demand for a specific amount, on a specific date, for named work. If any of those are fuzzy, the follow-up later will be fuzzy too.

  • Who you are. Name or studio, email, city. A street address is optional on the PDF.
  • Who pays. Client name, email, city. Match the name they use to pay.
  • Invoice number. Sequential. If you later add a late-fee revision, use 1042-A or a new line — do not reuse the number as if nothing changed.
  • Issue date and due date. “Net 15” in the notes is not a substitute for a calendar due date on the sheet. People pay dates, not jargon.
  • Line items. Description, quantity, rate in USD, amount. One idea per line. “Brand work” is how disputes start.
  • Tax. A percent if you collect sales tax on that job. Zero if you do not. Afterdue will not file it for you.
  • Payment instructions. The exact handle, email, or routing path. “Pay me whenever” is not an instruction.
  • Notes. Scope, revision rounds, PO number, and any late-fee sentence you already agreed.

The live generator has a field for each of those.

How to use the generator

  1. Open the invoice section. Sample data is already filled so you can see the shape — a studio in Austin billing a bakery in Brooklyn, invoice 1042, 8.25% tax, Net 15. Start blank if you want an empty sheet.
  2. Replace every field. Your name, their name, the real number, the real dates.
  3. Add or remove line items. Quantity times rate becomes the amount. The tax line and total update as you type. Check 50% deposit if this invoice is a start-work deposit.
  4. Write payment instructions the way you actually get paid. A usable shape: Zelle email, ACH on request, Venmo handle, “include the invoice number in the memo.”
  5. Print / Save PDF. In the browser print dialog, choose Save as PDF and US Letter. The PDF does not say Afterdue.

Start blank clears the demo and sets a Net-15 due date from today. Reset sample puts the demo back. A draft is saved in this browser so a refresh does not wipe it. Nothing is uploaded to Afterdue.

Tax, without a lecture

The tax field is a percent of the subtotal. Use it if you collect sales tax on that job. Leave it at zero if you do not. Afterdue is not a tax filing and not a nexus calculator. If you are unsure whether a particular client or state requires you to collect, that is an accountant question, not a template question.

Payment rails that fit on one sheet

US clients pay how they already pay vendors. Put one primary method first.

  • Zelle — an email or mobile number you control. Fast. No invoice platform in the middle.
  • ACH — bank name, account name, routing on request or on the sheet if you are comfortable.
  • Venmo — the handle, and a note if you need them to mark it as goods and services.

Afterdue does not process those payments. We do not send the invoice for you. The PDF is yours to attach.
